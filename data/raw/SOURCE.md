# Data source

These 30 CSV files are real used-car listings scraped from **riyasewana.com** (ads posted May–August 2026).
They come unchanged from the `dataset/` folder of the public repository
[savindu777/Sri-Lanka-Car-Market-Anlaysis-2026](https://github.com/savindu777/Sri-Lanka-Car-Market-Anlaysis-2026),
which is published under the MIT licence (as stated in that repository's README). Credit to its author for the scraping.

Only these raw files are reused. All cleaning, feature engineering, modelling, explanation, the app and the
MLOps pipeline in this repository are original work.

Prices are **asking** prices from classified ads, not final sale prices.
